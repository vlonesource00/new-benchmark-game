# PULSE replay and behavior review — 11–12 October 2026

PULSE has a fast, repeatable Harbor GTP motor and narrowly demonstrated learned
passes. The complete traffic controller remains unqualified: changing the last
GT3's arrival exposes rear-bumper contacts, and passed cars can accompany large
corrections and excursions when the road ahead is clear. Better strategy,
multiple tracks/classes and production browser workers remain separate gates.

## What the eye tests exposed

| Sampled trial | First contact | Closing speed | Own damage | Legal-limit time |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| c32 g0-n9 | 21.917 s | 1.20 m/s | 3.99% | 0.20 s |

| c32 g0-n1 | 22.050 s | 1.47 m/s | 1.30% | 0 s |

The first contacts are mild rear-quarter/nose impacts, with only 0.93–1.47 m
between centers laterally and about 4.56 m longitudinally. They are not verified
matched-speed side rubs. g0-n9 later has a 6.35 m/s impact at 26.775 s. Visual
stability therefore does not imply an undamaged pass. g0-n1 approaches the outer
edge beyond the asphalt but remains within this game's legal kerb allowance;
that does not make its trajectory desirable.

These c32 variants are timed offline control exploration. They are useful
targets, not evidence that the fitted NN already switches to the other side.
Only NN-only evaluations can establish that behavior.

## Line, controls and resources

The c34 motor's six changed starts and six solo delay/blackout cases remain
clean over 120 seconds, with full laps below 53 s. Its isolated motor is not the
main failure in the nominal traffic encounter.

In the new c53 nominal 60-second run, the first excursion starts at 40.32 s,
with the nearest car 144.6 m away and a steering correction of +0.365. The
changed-tail runs likewise show excursions with no car within 50 m ahead.
This identifies a traffic-branch issue to isolate; it does not assign fault
to a particular rival or prove every later excursion has the same cause.

The full c63 trace review finds 30 rapid executed pedal polarity reversals in
the 20–30 s window of nominal c35 traffic. The mean distance from the binned
solo line then grows from about 1.3 m in the 30–40 s window to 9.3 m in 40–50 s.
Departure during a useful pass is expected; departure on a clear road that
causes a cut or wall impact is not.

The pedal-layer review records policy proposals, the local learned motor and
executed controls separately. In the 20–30 s c35 traffic phase, 30 rapid
executed reversals accompany 30 front-obstruction guard rows. Those counts do
not prove that the guard causes every reversal: requests, delivered corrections
and local motor commands refer to different points in the mailbox cycle.
The clean c67 encounter has no guard intervention and no rapid executed
reversals in this phase. Avoid diagnosing the whole two-rate controller from
the proposal alone, or loosening the guard to conceal poor separation.

Tyre core temperatures, wheel wear, fuel and battery are recorded. These short,
soft-tyre encounters cannot establish compound choice, stint planning, pit
strategy, independent energy management or rain competence.

## Development changes and limits

- Tail-arrival fixtures vary only the last GT3's initial distance or speed;
  its actual opponent AI still reacts. Initial snapshots verify that the first
  two rivals and PULSE are unchanged. No forced blocking route is used.
- Contact diagnostics use the same 0.98 × 2.28 m collision-box half extents as
  the game. Geometry is record-only and does not drive the car.
- The optional 6,642-parameter NN calibration head preserves every parent
  weight, the empty-rival motor and same-observation memory. Total size is
  141,015 parameters. Legacy raw-gain checkpoints retain their original math;
  new calibrators match the offline post-clip actuator-delta contract.
- c52's low-rate calibration had not learned the desired gain: on the selected
  40–50 s states it delivered roughly 92–98%, where the safe target was 25%.
  c60 epoch 100 learns approximately 23–27% there. Its NN-only full encounter
  improves to 53.694 s to 2700 m, but still has 1.50% unverified damage and
  0.542 s of illegal track use. It is not promoted.
- Complete earlier/later-tail search encounters c58/c59 find legal, zero-damage
  alternatives that retain two traffic passes. These include offline sampled
  control changes and a late gain adjustment; they are training data, not a
  shipped tactical rule or autonomous NN certification.
- The c65 full-traffic refit learns the earlier-tail encounter without sampled
  controls: c67 and c68 both retain two passes, with zero damage and legal
  continuation through 60 s. c67 reaches 2700 m in 55.120 s. This reproduces a
  training encounter; nominal/later-tail and changed-gap cases still fail.
- c70 extends the c65 NN to 120 s. All five arrival variants fail complete
  admission. The earlier-tail case remains undamaged with two held passes, but
  cuts the legal edge for 0.317 s on the next lap at about 83 s. Its first-minute
  success therefore is not a complete long-run driving gate.
- c71 adds a new intermediate-tail training family: a full legal 60 s sampled
  winner retains two passes with a verified side rub (0.44% damage, 1.47 m/s
  closing speed, about 2.13 m forward/1.98 m lateral separation). c73 adds a
  distinct 90 s training family with two retained passes, legal track use and
  zero damage; it removes the traffic delta after 60 s offline. That timing is
  a data-generation intervention, not a shipped NN rule.
- The first 90 s recovery search used a 4800 m checkpoint that none of its
  otherwise legal gain trials reached. c73 explicitly uses 4500 m, reached in
  86.292 s. This is a fixture correction, not a faster NN. Its training family
  is distinct from the withheld evaluation seed.
- User eye test: c73's wait behind the last car, subsequent pass further along
  the circuit and clean continuation were judged a useful advancement. In the
  selected PULSE trial, the wait/pass precedes the offline adjustment at 60 s
  and is NN-generated. Preserve that behavior; the whole 90 s trial still
  requires a fitted-NN reproduction without the late gain intervention.
- Trainer sources are archived with each experiment. Same physical encounters
  cannot become independent holdouts through changed duration, update cadence,
  labels or explicit zero-valued tail settings.

late, fast and slow arrivals fail. The fast case's first large excursion at

nearest rival about 206 m away. Thus collision damage alone cannot explain
the clear-road failures. The slow/late cases do have earlier contacts.
c91 records full-rate line, pedal-layer and contact evidence for these cases.

Best full solo laps are 52.758/52.517 s versus matched INTERCEPT teacher laps
52.375/52.292 s. The 250/500 ms traffic blackouts are admitted with two retained
passes and verified side rubbing; sustained 30 Hz traffic and 16.667 ms reply
lag still fail. The latter accepts 3,600 replies and rejects 3,599 obsolete
replies: mailbox/state timing needs its own diagnosis before latency promotion.
This is native simulated scheduling, not proof under browser rendering load.

c92/c93/c94 now supply complete admitted fast/slow/late training examples.
c92's winner is zero-damage and legal for 90 s, but only one counted traffic
pass because the other cars became hazards. c93 has three held traffic passes

These are offline sampled outcomes, not yet a new fitted-NN accomplishment.
One c93 trial failed identical-prefix checks and was rejected.

### Gates for the next development round

1. Fit the admitted complete encounters and run the fitted NN without any
   sampled control or timed gain intervention. Judge fixed-epoch checkpoints
   as well as the minimum action-error checkpoint; MSE alone cannot promote one.
2. Require retained passes, legal continuation, no rear-bumper impact, acceptable
   documented side rubbing and sufficient fuel. Inspect actual lateral
   separation before the nose reaches the rival, not just final order.
3. Recheck changed gaps, earlier/later tail arrival, complete 120-second runs,
   30 Hz updates, sustained reply lag and 250/500 ms blackouts. Keep the earlier
   narrow passing checkpoint as a comparison if a new fit regresses.
4. Extend only validated NN behavior to healthy traffic trains, GTP duels,
   hazards, other tracks/classes, strategy and real game workers.
